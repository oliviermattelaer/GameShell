Mission goal
============

Last step: now that the job saves its progress before the walltime, it can also put itself back in the queue, and continue automatically until the computation is finished.

You are in the directory "~/Checkpoint/level7", which contains your scripts from level 6. "STOP" was reduced to 200 in "count_signal.py", so that the whole computation needs 2 or 3 jobs. Follow the comments at the top of "submit.sh":

  1. when the signal arrives and python has saved its progress, requeue the job with "scontrol requeue $SLURM_JOB_ID",
  2. add "#SBATCH --requeue".

Submit the job and let it run until the counter reaches the end: it should be requeued at least once, without your help.

Run "gsh check" once the job is COMPLETED. (The whole run takes about 5 to 10 minutes.)


Remarks
-------

A requeued job keeps its job ID. "sacct -D -X -j JOBID" shows all its runs ("-D" shows the runs that were requeued).

A requeued job does not restart immediately. It goes back to the queue, and Slurm usually keeps it PENDING for a while before it is allowed to start again ("squeue --me" shows the reason "BeginTime"): typically a minute or two, but it depends on the configuration of the cluster. Then it waits for a free node like any other job. Don't worry if nothing happens for a few minutes, and don't resubmit it!


Useful commands
===============

scontrol requeue JOBID
  Stop the job JOBID and put it back in the queue.

squeue --me
  List your jobs, and their state.

sacct -D -X -j JOBID
  Show all the runs of job JOBID.

tail -f FILE
  Display the end of FILE and follow it as it grows (Control-c to stop following).
