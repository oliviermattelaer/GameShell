Mission goal
============

You will not always be watching your jobs to send them a signal before the end of their walltime! Slurm can do it for you, with the option "--signal".

You are in the directory "~/Checkpoint/level5", which contains your scripts from level 4. To make this level quicker, the walltime of "submit.sh" was reduced to 70 seconds. The comments at the top of "submit.sh" explain how to use "--signal".

  1. Add the right "#SBATCH --signal=..." line to "submit.sh", so that Slurm sends SIGINT to python 60 seconds before the end of the walltime.
  2. Submit the job, and wait. Do not send any signal yourself!
  3. Check that the progress was saved in "checkpoint.txt".

Run "gsh check" once the job is finished.


Remarks
-------

The signal is sent to the job *steps*: python must be started with "srun".

Slurm only looks at its timers about once a minute: the signal can arrive up to a minute earlier than you asked.


Useful commands
===============

sbatch SCRIPT
  Submit the job described in SCRIPT.

squeue --me
  List your jobs, and their state.

tail -f FILE
  Display the end of FILE and follow it as it grows (Control-c to stop following).
